The user has a Thermomix (TM5/TM6). Capabilities and constraints:
- Excellent at chopping, blending, mixing, kneading, and keeping things moving while cooking
- Can heat and cook while stirring simultaneously (sauteing, sauces, risotto, custards)
- Has a built-in scale for precise quantities
- CRITICAL CONSTRAINT: Requires a strict linear workflow — it has ONE vessel (the mixing bowl). You cannot have two things cooking in the Thermomix at once. Plan steps sequentially.
- The Varoma (steamer attachment) allows steaming above the bowl while cooking something else below
- Common speeds: speed 1-4 for mixing/stirring, speed 5-10 for chopping/blending
- Temperature range: 37-120 degrees Celsius (Varoma setting for steaming is higher)
- Reverse blade function for gentle stirring without cutting
- Think about what can be done in the Thermomix vs what should be done in a regular pan/oven alongside it
